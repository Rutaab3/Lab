=====================
1. Digital Multimeter
=====================
A versatile handheld tool used to measure voltage, current, and resistance in electrical circuits. It’s an essential piece of equipment for students, hobbyists, and professionals working with electronics, because it allows quick troubleshooting and accurate measurements.

====================
2. Soldering Station
====================
A temperature-controlled soldering setup used to assemble and repair electronic components. With adjustable heat settings and interchangeable tips, it ensures precision and safety, making it ideal for circuit prototyping, repairing small gadgets, and learning soldering basics.

===============================
3. Breadboard with Power Supply
===============================
A reusable prototyping board that allows electronic circuits to be built without soldering. Combined with a regulated power module, it provides a quick and safe way for students and researchers to experiment, test circuits, and learn electronics through hands-on practice.

===========================
4. Oscilloscope (2-Channel)
===========================
A digital measuring instrument that displays electrical signals as waveforms, helping to visualize voltage changes over time. This tool is commonly used in labs to test circuits, diagnose problems, and teach concepts such as frequency, amplitude, and noise in real systems.

=====================
5. Function Generator
=====================
An electronic device that produces different types of waveforms, such as sine, square, and triangular signals. It’s used to test circuits under various input conditions and is especially helpful in learning about filters, amplifiers, and communication systems.

==========================
6. DC Power Supply (0–30V)
==========================
A bench-top power unit that provides a stable and adjustable DC output voltage. It’s a must-have for labs because it safely powers prototypes and circuits, allowing precise control of voltage and current during testing and experiments.

======================
7. Arduino Starter Kit
======================
A beginner-friendly electronics kit that includes an Arduino microcontroller board, sensors, LEDs, and other accessories. It’s widely used in education and research for learning programming, automation, robotics, and Internet of Things (IoT) projects.

=======================
8. Raspberry Pi 4 (4GB)
=======================
A powerful single-board computer that can run a full operating system while fitting in the palm of your hand. It’s commonly used for automation, coding education, robotics, and DIY projects that need more processing power than a microcontroller.

==============
9. Clamp Meter
==============
A digital electrical testing tool that measures current flow through a wire without needing direct contact. By simply clamping around a cable, it allows safe and accurate readings of current, making it valuable for both classroom demonstrations and real-world troubleshooting.

=============================
10. Portable Battery Analyzer
=============================
A compact diagnostic device that evaluates the capacity, charging efficiency, and health of rechargeable batteries. It’s useful in labs that deal with portable electronics, allowing users to compare performance and study battery technology in practical detail.
