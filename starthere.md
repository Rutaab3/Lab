This project is designed for **testing electrical gadgets** and **maintaining their records**.  

Managing records manually—hardcoded or on paper—is a **major headache**, and finding a specific record can feel **impossible**.  

The **SRS Lab Automation System** automates tasks, assigns **role-specific responsibilities**, and stores all information in a **centralized database** for easy access.  

In conclusion it makes managing and retrieving data.
